What is the MTFOA?

Tennessee’s premier secondary school football officiating association

The Middle Tennessee Football Officials Association is made up of individuals dedicated to the highest standards of training, professionalism, and knowledge.

Who do we service?

Middle school through varsity

MTFOA services secondary school programs in Davidson, Sumner, Williamson, Wilson, Cheatham, Robertson, Coffee, and Cannon counties.

How do I join the MTFOA?

Join as a reserve official

New and experienced officials are welcome. Reserve officials receive training that is second-to-none in both classroom and on-field settings.
