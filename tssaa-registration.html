Registering With the TSSAA on Arbiter

After you register with the MTFOA and pay your dues through PayPal, you will then need to register with the TSSAA by following the steps below. TSSAA registration for the upcoming season opens on June 1.

1. Visit https://tssaa.arbitersports.com and log in to your Arbiter account.

2. Click the blue Register button to begin (fig. 1).

(fig. 1)

3. Click through and complete all registration/consent steps. Be sure to select football under Sports and select both 25-26 season check boxes (fig. 2).

  • Select MTFOA for the association if it is not already auto-filled.
  • Verify all personal information and click Check Out.

(fig. 2)

4. A $70 registration payment is required using a VISA, MasterCard, or Discover. Once all information is entered, click Process Payment (fig. 3).

(fig. 3)

5. Once a successful payment is made, you will see a confirmation page — PRINT THIS PAGE FOR YOUR RECORDS. You will also receive registration and payment confirmation emails with the same information from messaging@arbitersports.com. The NFHS rule packet will arrive at your registered address via USPS mail in a few weeks as the season approaches.

6. You can revisit your Arbiter dashboard to check on the status of your TSSAA registration by clicking the Eligibility tab at the top (fig. 4).

(fig. 4)

7. If you have questions regarding your TSSAA registration, contact Kim Alley, kalley@tssaa.org
