Join & register

Joining the association, or renewing for the season, takes two separate registrations. Register with the MTFOA first, then with the TSSAA.

Step 1

MTFOA registration

Your annual membership in the Middle Tennessee Football Officials Association.

  • Use the same email you use or plan to use in Arbiter.
  • You pay online at the end of the form, by credit or debit card or by bank account, on Stripe’s secure payment page. The MTFOA never sees or stores your payment details.
  • Your membership is not valid until you have paid.

Registration is closed for now. Returning officials will be notified when it opens. New officials can sign up on the registration page for an email reminder.

Register with the MTFOA
Step 2

TSSAA registration

Your state registration, completed in your Arbiter account after you have registered with the MTFOA.

  • Opens each year on June 1.
  • Choose football as your sport and select the MTFOA as your association.
  • The step-by-step guide has screenshots of every screen.

      All dates, times and locations are subject to change, including because of activities at the host school. Local meetings are held at Goodpasture Christian School, 619 W. Due West Ave., Madison, TN 37115, unless noted.

      Please read: playoff eligibility

      To be eligible to work the playoffs, you need credit for six local meetings and the state meeting.

      Of the six required meetings, five must be in person. You may receive credit for one meeting by viewing one of the meeting recordings placed on the Member page. Go to Member Resources
